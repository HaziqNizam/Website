INTRODUCTION

Discover Bukit Batu Puteh

Bukit Batu Puteh is a natural area located in Kuala Perlis, Perlis. The area is rich in natural resources and features a variety of flora and fauna. It also has unique geological and biological features that make it an interesting site for nature and geotourism activities. The combination of its geological formations, biodiversity, flora, and fauna makes Bukit Batu Puteh a valuable natural area for exploration, education, research, and tourism.

Background Bukit Batu Puteh

Bukit Batu Putih, Kuala Perlis is a unique geological area featuring limestone formations and distinctive karst landscapes. The area offers scenic natural views and has significant geological, educational, and tourism value.

Besides its unique rock formations, Bukit Batu Putih is also home to various flora and fauna, as well as local historical and cultural values. These features make the area a potential destination for geological interpretation and nature-based tourism.

Therefore, the development of a Digital Interpretive Geotrail is proposed to provide visitors with a more interactive and engaging experience through digital elements such as maps, photographs, geological information, and interpretive content.

01

Archaeological Discovery

Archaeological findings at Bukit Batu Putih, including ceramic fragments linked to the Qing Dynasty, reveal the area’s historical significance and evidence of past human activities.

02

Piama Plant

Piama (Phyllanthus mirabilis) is a rare limestone plant found in Perlis, reflecting the unique biodiversity and Indo-Burma floristic influence of the region.

Site Protection

This restricted area is monitored by surveillance cameras to prevent unauthorized access and protect the site.

04

Stalactites and Stalagmites

The formations hanging from the cave ceiling are called stalactites, while stalagmites grow upward from the cave floor. The stalactites shown in the picture are natural limestone formations that develop over a long period of time through mineral-rich water flowing through the rock.
